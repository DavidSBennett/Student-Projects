    Roots of Resilience

    Historical Research

    This game explores Black sharecropping households, agricultural debt, and landownership in Georgia between 1877 and 1901.

    The storyline is a fictional reconstruction informed by historical research. Resource amounts are simplified gameplay values, not historical statistics.
